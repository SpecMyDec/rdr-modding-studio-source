## The direct test failed

If the asset names ever spelled a word out, that would settle
it. They do not: checked against every `.anim` and `.gait` name
on the disc, **not one of these abbreviations appears in full**.
There is no Rosetta stone in the corpus, so what follows is
inference rather than evidence.

## How accurate the generator is, measured

Run against the 24 abbreviations that already have independent
evidence, ranking candidates by how often the game displays them:

- right answer ranked **first: 6 of 24**
- in the top three: **7 of 24**
- target word in the game's vocabulary at all: **15 of 24** —
  the game never says "velocity", "rotation" or "transition",
  so no vocabulary drawn from it can ever reach those.

An earlier cut scored **24 of 24**. That number was worthless:
the word pool was one I had typed out myself and it contained
exactly the answers. Against the real vocabulary, with
distractors, it is 6.

## Slot anchors

Animation names are `_`-separated and positional. Each slot
holds a few unambiguous whole words that fix its category, and
a candidate that fits the category beats one that does not —
that is what separates `stn` = stand from `stn` = stunned.

- **slot 1**: `for`, `sit`, `rev`, `run`, `bod`
- **slot 2**: `run`, `for`, `rev`, `mid`, `min`, `max`
- **slot 3**: `run`, `mid`, `aim`, `for`, `hit`, `die`
- **slot 4**: `mid`, `aim`, `run`, `hit`, `for`
- **slot 5**: `aim`, `mid`, `run`, `hit`
- **slot 6**: `aim`, `run`
- **slot 7**: `aim`
