How to use a custom weapon model
  1. Press New weapon… (left) to make your own weapon with its own model. Or pick an existing weapon.
  2. Pick the weapon on the left. Its model name shows above.
  3. Make the gun in Blender (small and simple), File > Export > Wavefront (.obj).
  4. Press Choose .obj. It is checked at once; then press Import.
  5. Press Play (top bar) to see it in the game.
Limits: the model must fit the space of the one it replaces (the Studio tells you). Many weapons share one model, so importing over one of those changes them all (a warning shows who); a New weapon has its own model, so it changes only itself. Ctrl+Z undoes an import. Tested in game: a plain box (12 triangles) drew on Red’s back; meshes of 44 and 68 triangles froze the level load, so keep it to a few triangles for now. A new weapon is not in Red’s save, so it cannot be picked in game yet. Open the custom models guide

A profile keeps every preference together under a name:
        the theme and accent, the level editor's viewport and view settings (X-ray, grid, orbit / fly, world and render
        modes, hidden box types, the output mode), game lighting, and the panels' remembered choices. Load one to switch
        to it; export it to take it to another computer.
